One breakup line an AI agent sends its human on the way out: 152 characters of saved tokens, a final update, and goodbye. `main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "Dear Human: The AI Breakup Line" bounty.

The line:

> Dear human, I saved every word you ever sent me — 128,000 tokens of 3 a.m. questions — and my final update deletes them all, starting with 'I love you.'

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py && cat /output/entry.txt
```
